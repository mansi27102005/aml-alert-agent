# Synthetic AML Policy POL_STRUCT

## Cash deposits and potential structuring

This fictional internal policy is provided only for software demonstration and is not legal advice. Multiple cash deposits below an applicable reporting threshold may require review when their timing, aggregate value, customer profile, or lack of an evident business rationale suggests possible structuring. Do not infer intent from amount alone. Review the customer's expected activity, transaction timing, locations, and supporting records. Near-threshold patterns should be escalated for analyst review rather than automatically treated as suspicious. Record the exact transaction source IDs supporting every factual statement.

## Escalation

Escalate cases with repeated sub-threshold cash activity and no documented rationale to a senior AML analyst. Where the evidence is incomplete, request further information rather than inventing missing facts.
