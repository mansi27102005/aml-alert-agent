# Synthetic High-Value Transfer Policy POL_HVT

## High-value and unusual transfers

This fictional policy requires review of transfers that are unusual relative to the customer's expected activity, including large transfers to new counterparties or unfamiliar jurisdictions. Consider customer profile, transaction history, stated purpose, counterparty, and supporting documentation. A high amount alone does not establish suspicious activity.

## Documented rationale and missing evidence

A transfer supported by consistent invoices, contracts, or escrow documents may be recommended for false-positive closure after human review. If purpose or supporting records are missing, request information. Escalate when the pattern remains unexplained or material risk indicators conflict with the customer's known profile.
