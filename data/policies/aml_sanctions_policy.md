# Synthetic Sanctions Screening Policy POL_SANCT

## Name screening

This fictional policy describes a synthetic internal screening workflow. A name similarity score is a triage signal, not proof of identity. Compare available date of birth, nationality/country, and other identifiers. A high similarity score with corroborating identifiers must be escalated to a designated human sanctions reviewer. A close name with conflicting identifiers must not be automatically treated as a confirmed match. Automated closure is prohibited when identity evidence is incomplete or conflicting.

## Handling uncertain matches

For ambiguous matches, request additional identifying information or escalate for enhanced review. Never state that a person is sanctioned solely because their name resembles an entry in the synthetic watchlist.
